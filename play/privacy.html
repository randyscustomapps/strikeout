Privacy policy

Strikeout is published by Randy's Canadian Ltd, a Canadian company. This page is the privacy policy for the Strikeout app on Google Play and for the Strikeout website. Replace CONTACT_EMAIL with a real address before you give this URL to Play Console.

Strikeout runs on your phone. This is what it does with information, in plain words.

Location

Location is used for the optional Auto line advance, and for Screen set to Auto. Auto watches how far you have driven along the field so the current row can move after a real turnaround. That uses precise GPS while Auto is on and the app is open. Screen set to Auto uses location to choose day or night from sunrise and sunset. It does not use the light sensor. A coarse fix is enough for sunrise and sunset. If Auto is off and Screen is Day or Night, Strikeout does not use location.

Coordinates stay in memory on the phone. They are not written into the saved field setup, and they are not sent to Randy's Canadian Ltd or to anyone else. Strikeout does not track you in the background and does not share location with other people.

On this phone

Header sizes, crew counts, the current row, and the rest of your field setup stay on this phone. They are not uploaded. Updating the app refreshes the files and leaves that setup where it is. Reset everything, in Settings, clears that setup on this phone. There is no copy on a server to delete.

You can send a header list to another phone with the phone's own share sheet or a file. That send is started by you. Strikeout does not run a message service and does not see who you send it to.

No ads, no trackers

There are no ads and no trackers. Nothing is sent to a server — not your numbers, not your location, not a name. There is no account to sign in to. The app does not collect or sell personal information.

The only network requests the app itself makes are for its own files: the pages, the version check, and this policy. Those requests do not include your field setup or your location.

The Play Store app and the website

The Android app from Google Play is a one-time purchase. Google handles the payment. Randy's Canadian Ltd does not receive your card number. The website at randyscustomapps.github.io/strikeout stays free to open in a browser. It is the same calculator. Buying the Play app does not create an account on the website.
