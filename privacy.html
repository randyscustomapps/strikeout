Privacy

Strikeout Harvest is published by 1594818 B.C. LTD., Fort St. John, BC. This is what the app does with information, in plain words.

Subscription

The Play app is free to install. After a 3-day free trial it is $24.99 CAD a year, and it renews each year until you cancel. Google Play bills it and shows the price before you pay. We never see your card number.

Google Play tells the app on this phone whether the subscription is active, and gives it a purchase token. The token stays on the phone. It is not sent to us. Subscribing does not create an account.

To cancel, open the Play Store, then Payments & subscriptions, then Strikeout Harvest. Cancel there. Google Play manages the renewal.

On this phone

Header sizes, crew counts, the current row, and the rest of your field setup stay on this phone, in its local storage. They are not uploaded. Reset everything, in Settings, clears that setup on this phone. There is no copy on a server.

You can send a header list with the phone's own share sheet or a file. You start that send. Strikeout Harvest does not run a message service.

Location

Location is used only while Auto line advance is on, and while Screen is set to Auto. Auto watches how far you have driven so the current row can move after a real turnaround. Screen set to Auto uses location for sunrise and sunset. It does not use the light sensor. If Auto is off and Screen is Day or Night, the app does not use location.

Coordinates stay in memory. They are not saved with your field setup, they are not sent to us, and the app does not track you in the background.

No ads, no analytics

There are no ads and no analytics. There is no account. The app loads its own pages, the version check, and this policy. Those requests do not include your field setup, your location, or your purchase token.
