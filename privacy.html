Privacy

Strikeout runs on your phone. This is what it does with information, in plain words.

Location

Location is used only for the optional Auto line advance. Auto watches how far you have driven along the field so the current row can move after a real turnaround. If Auto is off, Strikeout does not use location.

On this phone

Header sizes, crew counts, the current row, and the rest of your field setup stay on this phone. They are not uploaded. Updating the app refreshes the files and leaves that setup where it is.

No ads, no trackers

There are no ads and no trackers. Nothing is sent to a server — not your numbers, not your location, not a name. There is no account to sign in to.
